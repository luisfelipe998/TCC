With respect to expression (32.12), it was mentioned that if no smoothing is
performed in time or scale domain over both numerator and denominator estimators,
then WCSyy (b, a) will result in a trivial unitary response because numerator and
denominator of (32.12) will be equal [ToCo98, MaKu04, MaEtA107]. This was one
of the reasons to suggest a smoothing separately for numerator and denominator in
order to solve this flaw.
